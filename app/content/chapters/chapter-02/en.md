## Chronology

1. An essay on silence. [[mari]] wakes on a hard medical couch — in a white sundress, without a scratch.
2. Around her: an endless black glossy floor “like dry water”, light from nowhere. Absolute, echoless silence — she can hear her own blood.
3. Panic, grief. Mari spins with her eyes closed, picks a direction and walks for who knows how long.
4. A white point appears — it rushes at her like a train and floods everything with white. The [[transition]] makes her dizzy.
5. The clatter of a typewriter. A round 1970s veneer desk emerges from the white, and behind it [[morana]], [[transition-registrar]].
6. A memorised greeting: welcome to [[universalis]], [[purification-department]]. Mari has violated **seven points** of the [[charter]] and will undergo matching [[trials]]; a [[reports|report]] will be written and analysed by the [[moral-evaluation-department]].
7. A stamp comes down. The folder is to be handed to “your purification manager”.
8. A black star — the world flips back into darkness. Before a row of folding wooden seats stands a lone [[doors|door]].
9. Inside the folder: a [[case-file|copy of her file]] — a red “Dead” stamp on her photo, a blue “Certified true copy” beside it.

## What we learn about the world

- The procedure for registering and “purifying” a soul.
- Transitions between spaces — the white point and the black star.
- Freestanding doors as portals.
- Aesthetic: typewriters, stamps, queues — 1970s bureaucracy.
