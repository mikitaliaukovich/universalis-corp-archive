## Chapter outline

1. The [[archivist]] finishes [[mari]]'s book and is shaken: it is the first book ever changed after being written — an [[altered-book]]. Handwritten edits, most events blacked out.
2. The handwritten ending says Mari will be made to do something terrible on someone else's orders without even realising it.
3. He resolves to warn her and stop the villain — but fears the world beyond the library.
4. For the first time he wonders: how did he get here, and who is he really?
5. He remembers the book mentioned an Archive episode with [[doors]]. He puts the book in the clutch he carries books in and goes looking for a door.
