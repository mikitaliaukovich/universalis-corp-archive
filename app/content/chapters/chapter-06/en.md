## Chronology

1. [[mari]] stumbles out of a toilet stall in a dirty bar with a stranger's hoarse voice and decides purgatory was a trip.
2. [[derek]] hands her a “Submarine” — a shot of vodka dropped into beer.
3. In the bar: a cowboy with a bodyguard and a girl holding a chihuahua, athletes toasting their star, and a priest.
4. [[jasmine]] laughs at “husband and son” and calls her Jess. In the mirror — a stranger. Mari has [[possession|possessed]] [[jess]].
5. The [[pager]] beeps: “IT'S ABOUT TO HAPPEN. FATHER J. DON'T LET HIM LEAVE. YOU MUST…” — the signal from purgatory is weak. Mari realises everyone is speaking English — the [[universal-language]].
6. She decides “Father J.” is [[father-jeremy]]. They argue about purgatory; he quotes Corinthians and finally admits: “I don't know.”
7. The crowd chants “Fa-ther!” — it's quarterback [[jay-abrams]]. Teammate [[ron]] slips powder into his drink.
8. Mari rushes over, collides with a waitress, cracks her head and briefly “wakes” in the black void. Jeremy, a former army doctor, patches her up.
9. Jay collapses, realises what Ron did and beats him to death. He runs to the bridge. With gallows humour, Mari talks him off the railing.
10. A gaunt Englishman in black — the [[reaper]] — calls her by name: he collected her parents. The pager is his.
11. Jay died of an overdose back in the bar. It's his soul walking beside Mari — and she “caught” it, keeping it from becoming [[restless-souls|restless]].
12. :redact[This man matters to “our plan”: the Reaper was busy, so Charon gave Mari the pager to temporarily wield Death's powers.]{ch=6}
13. “Come on, fly” — the Reaper leads the soul away and vanishes: “Don't lose my pager.” Dawn. The pager vibrates again.

## What we learn about the world

- The first of the [[trials]] — Pride.
- “Whoever holds the tools of Death is Death.”
- Hosts later remember only a blackout.
- Fate versus judgement: “the fly is judged for its deeds before”.
