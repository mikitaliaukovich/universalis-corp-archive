## Chronology

1. Sunset on a terrace in [[manarola]]. [[mari]], in a tourist's body, eats lasagne, drinks a spritz and chats to a stray dog she calls [[polygraph]].
2. Inside the dog is a human soul: Mari learned to [[possession|put souls into animals]] on her third job, when she angrily shoved a rude woman who'd choked on a kebab into a dog.
3. Recalling: after Dallas she followed the pager's instructions through a door to purgatory, where [[charon]] waited in a corridor (“we didn't have time to test the technology”). Why her, when Death has a huge staff?
4. She was taught to write reports; the second job came less than a day after Dallas. Mari wonders whether to stay on and work after the seventh.
5. She leaves her [[hosts|host]] and steps into the [[spirit-world]] — grey, scarred, littered with the trash of many eras, down to a faded anti-fascist leaflet.
6. On her second try she opens a fisherman's-hut [[doors|door]] onto a dark room with a velvet armchair — kinder than her own hospital couch — and seats Johan's soul there ([[first-moment]]).
7. Through the portal — into the offices. A minute of crying alone.
8. Time maths: [[purgatory-time|1 real minute = 1 purgatory hour]]. A half-hour dinner cost 30 hours; each job allows 3 days — 72 real minutes.
9. The concrete [[exit-room]] with five doors; the orange lines of the [[color-corridors|corridors]] lead to the red [[computing-hall]].
10. Runner [[alba]] joins her. They meet [[peter]], sent around by the [[engineers]] for two days; the gossip: the [[transistors|transistor]] in cubicle 4015 “burned out”, his cases will go to 1001.
11. Down the blue “To the Archive” corridor to the green door. [[friedrich]] takes Alba's books and fills in [[transfer-forms]]. No one knows of Charon's rebellion.
12. Down to [[mord]]: Mari punches her [[time-card]] at exactly 8:00, sits in an office the size of a mop closet and writes a [[reports|report]] on catching Johan Lindström from Gothenburg.

## What we learn about the world

- How the [[computing-hall]] works, the role of [[chief-scribe|Thoth]], the castes of [[runners]] and transistors.
- Staff work off their sins through labour.
- The rules of a soul's first moment and the [[manual]] for new catchers.
