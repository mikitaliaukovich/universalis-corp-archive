## Chronology

1. [[mari]] rests her head on the desk in [[mord]] — [[soul-projection|purgatory needs no sleep]]. A 10-page report is done.
2. Runner [[li]] knocks with a folder and a heavy accent. He tries to retell a rumour about a female Angel of Death who pulled souls out of the sea where there were no doors. It seems to be about Mari.
3. She deftly gets rid of him, slipping him her report “to take to Charon”, and takes the new folder.
4. She punches her card and goes up to the [[exit-room]] (“four doors and a window”).
5. [[beacons]]: objects linked to a person that let a non-reaper possess them. The possession itself is pre-computed in the books of fate.
6. An old worn key in her hand. Mari is ready.

:::classified{ch=9 title="Outline of the fifth trial"}
- A Swiss train, an abnormal spring blizzard. Mari steps out of the toilet. The target dies of anaphylactic shock.
- Having died fighting, the soul flees and [[possession|possesses]] someone else. Mari has to switch bodies to get through carriage doors while the target runs across the train roof through the storm.
- The rule of [[doors]]: a door opens to a place you hold an object or a description of. The object arrives by [[pneumatic-tube]] and must be returned. The [[catch-contract]] is signed and sent back the same way.
- The [[archivist]], reading the book, sees small “turbulences of fate” smooth out. He reveals himself and helps.
- What he knows makes it clear: [[charon]] and his accomplices are preparing a [[coup|military coup]] in purgatory — and must be stopped.
:::
