## Chronology

1. [[mari]] rests her head on the desk in [[mord]] — [[soul-projection|purgatory needs no sleep]], but the mind still tires. A ten-page report is done.
2. Runner [[li]] knocks with a folder and a heavy accent. He retells the story Mari already heard from Alba: an Angel of Death — “some girl” — pulling souls straight out of the sea where there wasn't a single door, swimming and swimming.
3. She deftly gets rid of him, slipping him her report “to take to Charon”, and takes the new folder.
4. She punches her [[time-card]] and goes up to the [[exit-room]] (“four doors and a window”).
5. [[beacons]]: an object tied to a person physically or emotionally — a cross, a rosary, a wedding ring, a dog's collar. Only reapers move freely (and [[morana]], who seems all-powerful here); Mari, a nobody with a borrowed sliver of power, has only a beacon. The possession itself is pre-computed in the [[computing-hall]] and written into the [[books-of-fates]], which is why beacons come with the assignment.
6. In her hand — an old worn key. Whose it is, and how beacons reach purgatory, she doesn't know. She closes her eyes and steps through the portal.
7. The door clicks shut: the toilet of an express train. For the first time her [[hosts|host]] is a man — greying, unshaven, about fifty, green eyes, a burgundy sweatshirt. Her reaction to the new body is heartfelt.
8. An announcement: because of the freak weather the Gotthard base tunnel is closed, and the eleven-car Milan–Zurich train is taking the panoramic route through the Alps, arriving an hour late. Outside — a blizzard and black-and-white cliffs.
9. Again no briefing: the report must be filed in detail, but who to look for is her problem. Mari has seventy-two minutes to find the soon-to-be-dead, wait for the death, catch the soul and return. She must not interfere: any action changes a fate. She heads for the rear of the train.
10. “Daddy!” — five-year-old [[nora|Nora Keller]] has hold of her finger. The girl's mother is “on a cloud, with grandma”; her father [[markus|Markus]] is her only parent, and the child cannot simply be abandoned in a carriage: every option would pull a chain of events after it.
11. Mari remembers Viktor at that age — Disneyland, the mountains, weekends in Italy — and takes Nora along, first rehearsing what she is to tell the conductor. This is the fifth soul of seven.

- The [[archivist]] reads the book alongside the events and wants to intervene, but the small turbulences of fate smooth themselves out. So he reveals himself to Mari and helps her.
- What he knows completes the picture: [[charon]] and some of his followers are preparing a [[coup|military coup]] in Purgatory — and they are what must be opposed.
