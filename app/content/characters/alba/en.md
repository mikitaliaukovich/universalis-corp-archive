## File

A slim, curly-haired blonde in a grey pencil-skirt suit and heels. Born in France in 1913, she has spent twenty real years as a [[runners|runner]] — working off her sins through physical labour.

She carries books of fate to the [[archive]] and hears about everything happening in the [[computing-hall]] before anyone else. She adores gossip: it is from her and [[peter]] that Mari learns about the burned-out [[transistors|transistor]] in cubicle 4015 ([[chapter-08]]).

## Friendship with Mari

They tease each other as “darling” and “old lady”. Mari doesn't hide the deal with [[charon]], but she doesn't tell the whole of what her work involves either, keeping back the details that would raise questions; of Charon's “little rebellion” nobody at all seems to know.
