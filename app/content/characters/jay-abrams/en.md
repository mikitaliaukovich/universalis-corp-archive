## File

A swarthy athlete about to make the national team: the trials are tomorrow and he honestly tries to stay sober, until the crowd pushes him into “just one” glass. The team calls him “Father” — for his leadership and his care for them — but he himself has long looked down on them as second-rate.

Teammate [[ron]] slips powder into his drink. Realising it, Jay smashes a glass into Ron's head and beats him to death, then runs to the bridge. [[mari]] talks him down — and only then does the [[reaper]] explain that Jay died of an overdose back in the bar, and what she saved was his soul ([[chapter-06]]).
