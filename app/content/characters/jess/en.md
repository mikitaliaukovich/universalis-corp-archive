## File

Stepping out of a toilet stall with a stranger's hoarse voice, [[mari]] sees an unfamiliar woman in the mirror — Jess. Her friend [[jasmine]] has known her for over twenty years. By the talk at the table, Jess was planning a trip of her own — Amsterdam comes in handy for Mari. After Mari leaves, Jess sobs out the impossible to her friends: something got inside her, made her say and do strange things, then let go — and she came to standing over two bodies in a pool of blood ([[chapter-06]]).
