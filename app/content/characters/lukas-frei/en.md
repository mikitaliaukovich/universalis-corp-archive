## File

The target of [[mari]]'s fifth assignment: he is meant to die of suffocation. Mari searches for him among the passengers until train manager [[mayer]] explains that there is no passenger by that name on board — Lukas Frei is driving the train.

When Mayer brings Mari to the cab, the door bursts open on its own: blue in the face and clutching his throat, Lukas stumbles into the car and drops dead in the aisle ([[chapter-09]]).

## The runaway soul

A soul that dies fighting for life won't let itself be caught: in a panic it [[possession|possesses]] the screaming [[nora]] and sends the girl running through the whole train. By the time the train goes off the bridge, Lukas is no longer in Nora — the fifth soul is never delivered to Purgatory.
