## File

A tall, strongly built man. His face, covered with a neat thick beard, looks bright and kind, and his hazel-green eyes tired and clever. Fifteen years of shared history with [[mari]] — including a three-month RV trip across the states a year before their son was born.

Mark endured her breakdowns for a long time, but after Mari put their son in a cast he agreed to a divorce — [[viktor]] himself asked for it, wanting to stay with his father and leave to study in a year. Seeing his wife's pink roadster, Mark calls [[rafael]] “Donatello”; Mari quietly corrects him: “Rafael”.

## Death

The papers were signed in a shopping-mall parking garage. Minutes later Mari's car broke through the wall one floor above. Mark managed to push his son aside — but could not save him ([[chapter-01]]).
