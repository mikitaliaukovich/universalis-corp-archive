## File

A tall, strongly built man. His face, covered with a neat thick beard, looks bright and kind, and his hazel-green eyes tired and clever. Fifteen years married to [[mari]].

Mark endured her breakdowns for a long time, but after Mari broke their son's arm he agreed to a divorce — [[viktor]] himself asked for it. It was Mark who first nicknamed [[rafael]] “Donatello”.

## Death

The papers were signed in a shopping-mall parking garage. Minutes later Mari's car broke through the wall one floor above. Mark managed to push his son aside — but could not save him ([[chapter-01]]).

## After death
