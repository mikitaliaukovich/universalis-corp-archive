## File

A tall, trim man of about fifty: pale stubble, grey hair over still-dark brows, expressive green eyes, a burgundy sweatshirt and classic blue jeans. Father of five-year-old [[nora]] and, by his daughter's account, her only parent.

[[mari]] lands in his body through a [[beacons|beacon]] — an old worn key — inside the toilet of a moving train. Until then she had only ever been given female bodies, which makes her first reaction to the new one entirely heartfelt ([[chapter-09]]).
