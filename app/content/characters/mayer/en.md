## File

A short, middle-aged man in the navy-blue uniform of the Swiss Federal Railways. The dining-car barman tracks him down over the intercom in the family car, where Mayer is chatting away with passengers ([[chapter-09]]).

[[mari]], in [[markus|Markus Keller]]'s body, invents a bank card found in the toilet and refuses to leave it with Mayer: it has to be handed over in person. Hearing the owner's name — [[lukas-frei|Lukas Frei]] — the train manager raises an eyebrow: that's the driver. In the end he gives in and leads “Herr Keller” and his daughter to the head of the train — just in time for the driver's death.
