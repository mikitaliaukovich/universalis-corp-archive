## File

The first face a newly arrived soul sees. Behind a round 1970s veneer desk, to the clatter of a typewriter, Morana recites a memorised greeting: “Welcome to [[universalis]], [[purification-department]]…” — and asks you not to hold up the queue.

A plump woman near retirement age in horn-rimmed glasses, with a styled dark bob, burgundy lipstick and a large dark wart above her lip. At the desk: pearl earrings, a brown turtleneck and a blue cardigan; in her office: a white high-neck sweater and silver hoops.

## Many faces

Morana serves as [[transition-registrar]] and senior [[purification-manager]] at the same time. Mari sees her at reception and moments later in an office between a white and a black filing cabinet. Here, in Purgatory, she is “all-powerful”.

:redact[She may well be the “she” whose reforms and optimisations stripped [[charon]] of power over soul logistics. The text only implies it.]{ch=4}

## Name

The name refers to Morana, the Slavic goddess of death and winter.
