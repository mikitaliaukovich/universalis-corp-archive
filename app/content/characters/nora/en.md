## File

A girl of about five in bright lilac tights, a lemon-yellow dress and a striped jumper, carrying a teddy bear with a pocket for sweets. She is travelling with her father [[markus]] on the Milan–Zurich express.

When [[mari]] steps out of the toilet in her father's body, Nora grabs her finger and calls her daddy. The girl has no mother — “she's on a cloud, with grandma” — and Mari, remembering her own son at that age, cannot bring herself either to abandon the child or to hand her to a stranger: any option would pull an unstoppable chain of events after it ([[chapter-09]]).

Before moving on, they rehearse the answer for the conductor: “Nora Keller, five years old”, and daddy's name is Markus.

Mari, to her own surprise, grows attached to the girl: in Nora she sees her younger self, not yet acquainted with loss or grief. With her penguin backpack and her teddy bear, Nora skips after “daddy” to the front of the train, hoping to get to honk the driver's horn.

## The crash

Nora watches the suffocating driver [[lukas-frei|Lukas Frei]] drop dead, and his panicking soul jumps into her — she runs screaming through the whole train. When the train goes off the bridge, she comes to as herself, in a car hanging over the drop. A falling man drags her by the leg towards the abyss; Nora kicks out his eye, and Mari, in her father's body, pushes her up to safety.

She cannot be saved: a freight train shoves the whole train into the gorge, and everyone dies ([[chapter-09]]). Hers is one of the deaths that, according to the [[archivist]], were never in the plan.
