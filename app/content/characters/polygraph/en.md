## File

A sad-eyed mutt on a terrace in [[manarola]]. [[mari]] named him “Polygraph” as a joke — a silent listener.

Inside the dog, Johan Lindström's soul waits its turn. Mari learned to [[possession|put souls into animals]] on her third job. Later she leads Johan through the [[spirit-world]] into a dark room with a velvet armchair — a kinder [[first-moment]] than her own hospital couch ([[chapter-08]]).
