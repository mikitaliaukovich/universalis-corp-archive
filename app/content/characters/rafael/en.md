## File

Athletic, with a fake tan, an unnaturally white smile and grey-blue eyes. Poses as an Italian though his roots are East Slavic; trained as an actor. Named after his mother's favourite sweets (Raffaello) and his father's favourite Ninja Turtle.

Works at a detailing studio and “borrows” clients' cars — he picks [[mari]] up from therapy in someone else's Ferrari. Later jailed for a scheme defrauding rich women that Mari dragged him into. [[mark]] calls him “Donatello”.
