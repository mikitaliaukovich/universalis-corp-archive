## File

In Mari's words — “a stranger with a strong British accent… dressed all in black: a black wool coat over an emaciated body, leather gloves sitting on bony fingers like latex”. A long dark bob slicked tight along a thin skull; cheekbones you could cut yourself on.

An [[reapers|Angel of Death]] catches a soul at the moment the body dies so it does not remain restless, and leads it to the other world. He can move freely between worlds while staying unseen.

## Meeting on the bridge

At dawn near a Dallas bridge he stops Mari, calls her by name, and says he collected her parents “that night” while she sat in the police station. He explains that [[jay-abrams]] already died in the bar — the Jay walking beside Mari is his soul, and she “caught” it ([[chapter-06]]).

:redact[This man matters to “our plan”. The Reaper was busy, so Charon gave Mari his pager to lend her Death's powers for a while.]{ch=6} Leading Jay's soul away — “Come on, fly” — he fades, with a parting “Don't lose my pager.”
