## File

Dressed deliberately severely: stylish black-framed glasses highlight her perceptive green eyes, a pencil skirt, a slightly unbuttoned blouse. Assigned to [[mari]] by court order after the “incident” with her son.

In [[chapter-01]] Mari arrives straight from a wild night, in a filthy sweater, and defends her right to self-destruct. The therapist suggests the self-destruction is a way of proving to herself and everyone else that her mother no longer controls her, and sets her homework: to find her own answers to her own questions. This is where the themes of atonement and control first appear.
