## File

Son of [[mari]] and [[mark]]. A schoolboy who races karts seriously — his best lap is 1:48. Dreams of studying abroad.

Viktor is in a cast: during his mother's drunken fight with a neighbour woman he tried to separate them, and Mari shoved him straight into the road. It was Viktor who insisted his father file for divorce — he wants to stay with Mark and leave to study in a year. When Mari comes home wasted, he is the one who puts her to bed.

## Death

Killed in the mall parking garage together with his father when Mari's car fell on them from above ([[chapter-01]]). His death is the main reason Mari will agree to any deal.
