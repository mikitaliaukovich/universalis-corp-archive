:::classified{ch=9 title="The Meeting"}
The Archivist shows the book to [[mari]] herself: her full name in silver on the cover, her whole life inside, from her first cry to her parents' death. The last legible episode is her car falling onto Mark and Viktor; after that the pages are covered in black bars. According to the [[archivist]], neither Mari nor her family were supposed to die like that. Who rewrote the fate, and how, he doesn't know — but her death was the first domino in a chain of unplanned deaths.
:::
