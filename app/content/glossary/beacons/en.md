A beacon is an object tied to a particular person physically or emotionally: a cross worn round the neck, a rosary, a wedding ring, even a favourite dog's collar. Holding one lets you possess that person and do your duty.

Only reapers (and, as Mari observes, the all-powerful [[morana]]) can cross between worlds at will; [[mari]] herself — a nobody with a borrowed sliver of the Angel of Death's power — has no other option. The possession itself is pre-computed in the [[computing-hall]] and written into the [[books-of-fates]] of everyone involved, which is why beacons are issued together with the assignment.

Before her fifth job Mari holds an old, worn key. Whose it is, and how beacons reach purgatory at all, she doesn't know ([[chapter-09]]).
