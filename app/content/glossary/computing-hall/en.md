A “human anthill” to the horizon: numbered cubicles (4015, 1001…), dim white light from nowhere, the clatter of keys and heels, clicking pens and staplers, cursing over the partitions.

One folder per person. [[transistors]] compute fates, [[runners]] carry papers between cubicles, and the [[chief-scribe]] performs the final check from a glass balcony above the entrance.
