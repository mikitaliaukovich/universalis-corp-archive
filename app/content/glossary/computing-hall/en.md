A “human anthill” to the horizon: numbered cubicles (4015, 1001…), dim white light from nowhere, the clatter of keys and heels, clicking pens and staplers, cursing over the partitions.

One folder per person. The department's least favourite category of events is travel and new acquaintances: every crossing of paths outside a person's immediate circle sends a wave through other fates, and everything has to be recomputed. [[transistors]] compute fates, [[runners]] carry papers between cubicles, and the [[chief-scribe]] performs the final check from a glass balcony above the entrance.
