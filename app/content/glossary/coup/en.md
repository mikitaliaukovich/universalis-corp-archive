In [[mari]]'s own words: [[charon]] wants to seize power in Purgatory, and her seven assignments are meant to help him. The [[reaper]], who speaks of “our plan”, is in on it too.

The [[archivist]] sees more in it than a change of management. Rewritten [[books-of-fates]] make people die before their time: [[jay-abrams|Jay Abrams]] was meant to die five years later, and the passengers of the Milan–Zurich train not at all. Every unplanned death, like the flap of a butterfly's wing, sets off more, and without precise computation [[morana]]'s system will collapse: wars, epidemics, famine, millions of deaths. “An apocalypse, I suppose.”

In the Archivist's view there is only one way to stop it — to kill Charon.
