First seen as a lone door in the middle of the void ([[chapter-02]]). To open a door where you need it, you picture the destination; Mari rarely manages it on the first try — “you have to live here a while longer for that”.

Reapers and higher entities walk through doors at will; [[mari]] returns to purgatory from memory, but reaches the world of the living only with a [[beacons|beacon]], out of the [[exit-room]].
