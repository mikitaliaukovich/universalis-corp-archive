Mari's first host was [[jess]] in Dallas; then a tourist in [[manarola]], and :redact[a small girl's father, [[markus|Markus Keller]], on a Swiss train — her first male body, which died with the train]{ch=9}. When the soul leaves, the host comes to with blurred memories: details and motives slip away, leaving only a sense of having blacked out.

A catcher in someone else's body works quietly and avoids contact where possible: any interference can change the target's fate, and that fate is already computed and written down.

:redact[Leaving a host before it dies is vital: if the body dies with the guest inside, the guest stays in it forever (see [[possession]]).]{ch=9}
