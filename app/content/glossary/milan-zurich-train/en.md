A white-and-red arrow from Milan to Zurich, eleven cars long. Freak weather has closed the Gotthard base tunnel, so the train is sent along the old mountain line of the panoramic route and will reach Zürich HB an hour late. Outside — a blizzard and black-and-white cliffs that erase the line between sky and ground.

This is where [[mari]] arrives by way of the key-[[beacons|beacon]], inside the body of [[markus|Markus Keller]] ([[chapter-09]]). Walking the whole train takes five to ten minutes; finding one particular person in it at the right moment is another matter.

The train is a Swiss Giruno: a dining car, a family car papered with cartoon characters, a business car and the “quiet” car at the head, next to the driver's cab. The train manager is [[mayer|D. Mayer]].

:redact[Driver [[lukas-frei|Lukas Frei]] suffocates in his cab, and his runaway soul sends five-year-old [[nora]] running through the whole train. On the last turn of a double spiral tunnel the train derails, smashes through a bridge parapet and hangs over the gorge; a freight train coming out of the tunnel behind it shoves it over. Everyone dies — although, according to the [[archivist]], it “isn't in the plans”.]{ch=9}
