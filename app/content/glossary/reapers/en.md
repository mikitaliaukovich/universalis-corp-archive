A soul left uncaught remains [[restless-souls|restless]]. An Angel of Death moves freely between worlds while unseen, and shapes a soul's [[first-moment]].

“Whoever holds the tools of Death is Death”: given the [[pager]], [[mari]] temporarily gains a Reaper's powers.

By Mari's reckoning the staff must be enormous — hundreds of thousands die on Earth every day — yet she never sees a reaper. Their work surfaces only in runners' gossip: one of them “got lucky” pulling souls one after another out of a sunken bathyscaphe, with the nearest [[doors|door]] on a ship four kilometres away.
