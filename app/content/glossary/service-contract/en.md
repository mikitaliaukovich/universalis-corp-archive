*“CONTRACT OF PAID SERVICE. Concluded between the Administration of Purgatory, represented by \_\_\_, and the soul \_\_\_. Subject of the contract: \_\_\_. Payment: \_\_\_”* — this is the form [[charon]] holds out to [[mari]], with blank lines where everything essential should be.

Spoken aloud, the payment is “granting the Contractor the right to distribution to Paradise upon fulfilment of obligations”; Mari catches on the word “right”. She refuses to sign an empty sheet, and Charon has neither the time nor the patience: “I can't stand this place's love of paperwork anyway. Here, I'll write on the way.” That time the contract is left unfilled and unsigned ([[chapter-04]]).

:redact[Mari signs it only after returning from Dallas. The [[archivist]] spots the catch at once: it is not an employment contract but a one-off arrangement. Mari is not on the Corporation's staff, she exists in no system, and [[morana]] tracks neither her work nor her purification — not until Mari comes back for distribution ([[chapter-09]]).]{ch=9}
