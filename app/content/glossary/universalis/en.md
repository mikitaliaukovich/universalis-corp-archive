The sign under which the afterlife bureaucracy greets new arrivals. Its emblem is a trident. The Corporation employs entities from different pantheons: Slavic [[morana]], Greek [[charon]], Egyptian [[thoth]].

Outwardly the Corporation is a secret concrete complex of the 1970s–80s: typewriters, pneumatic mail, pagers, rubber stamps and endless queues. The motto of the era: “Every sneeze is recorded.”
